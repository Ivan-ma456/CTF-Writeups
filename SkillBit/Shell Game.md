# Challenge Information:
**Challenge Name:** Shell Game

**Category:** Reconnaissance

# Challenge Description:
I was trying to track some political donations, only to discover that everything is done through a shell game of dark money. What is the name of the CEO of the organization that donated the most money to the Senate Leadership Fund in 2023-2024?

Note: The flag for this challenge is not in the traditional flag format. Just submit the CEO's name.

# Analysis:
The goal of this challenge is to find the CEO of the organization that donated the most money to the Senate Leadership Fund in 2023-2024. Copying and pasting the question in the challenge description as a search query on the internet gives the answer `Steven Law`, and further research into public finance records helped to verify Steven Law is indeed the CEO of the organization who donated the most to the Senate Leadership Fund in 2023-2024, so it is indeed the correct flag.

**flag:** `Steven Law`

# Lesson Learned:
- Publicly available information can often be uncovered quickly using well-targeted search queries.
- OSINT and reconnaissance challenges frequently depend more on finding reliable sources than on technical exploitation.
